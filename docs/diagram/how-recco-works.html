How Recco works

Text Recco the shows people tell you about. Your Fire TV keeps the list, who recommended each one, and what they said.

1
Install the Fire TV app
Get Recco on your Fire TV and open it.
2
Connect your number
Text the code on your TV to Recco to link your phone. No password needed.
3
Text it anything, like a friend
Say who told you about a show, paste a link, or describe a mood.
4
The Recco Agent handles it
It reads each text, looks it up, sorts it, and gets everything ready for your inbox.
5
Your Recco Inbox
Each show comes with your friend’s note and its ratings, in one organized list, so you can just tell Alexa what to watch.
