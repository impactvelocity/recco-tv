# 60-second ad script

Female voiceover, first person. For a TV spot or TikTok. The full demo video script is in `demo-script.md`.

9pm. Kids are down. This is my time.
Tea, a snack, my dog, and a really good show.

That's the plan.

But after ten minutes of looking, I check my phone for that show a friend mentioned.
And suddenly there's no time left for an episode, let alone a movie.
So I just keep scrolling.

The thing is, my best shows come from friends. I just never remember what they told me.

So now I use Recco.

Setup is easy. Install the Recco app on Fire TV, then text the code to the Recco number.
Now I can text it anything, and the Recco agent looks it up and gets it ready for me.

Jill says a sitcom is hilarious. I text it, and it's on my TV.
Steph sends a video with three shows. One link, three reccos.

Now when it's my time, my list is waiting.
All I have to do is tell Alexa what I want to watch.
And my phone stays in the other room.

Recco. From a friend, a link, or you. Ready when you are.
