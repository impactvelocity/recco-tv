Recco: TV screens

A wireframe map of the Fire TV app, drawn from the spec (R7) and the current code in apps/tv/src/screens. Four screens: Pairing, Inbox, Card detail, Settings. Each one has a few states. Boxes show what is on screen. They are not a design.

thick outline = where focus startsdashed box = an area of content

Flow

First launch ──► PAIRING (shows a code) ──she texts the code──► "Connected" ──► INBOX INBOX ──Select a card──► CARD DETAIL ──Back──► INBOX (same card keeps focus) INBOX ──Settings button──► SETTINGS ──Back / "Back to the list"──► INBOX INBOX ──Back──► exits the app Later launches skip Pairing and open the Inbox. Running in the background: live updates (a new text shows a placeholder card), plus the "offline" note.

1. Pairing

The first thing she sees. It connects this TV to her phone, and nothing on it takes focus. She texts the code from her phone and the TV moves on without her touching the remote.

Row order (fixed): New → Starred → one row per friend → From me → From the internet → Check these
  • Hero: title, year/type, who sent it, their track record, her note, where to watch. For a "Check these" card it says "3 possible matches · open it to pick". For an unmatched note it says "Not matched to a show yet".
  • Friend rows show the track record in the label ("3 of 4 landed").
  • Placeholder card ("Reading your text…", pulsing) appears in New when a text arrives and is swapped for the real card when the agent finishes. It never takes focus.
  • A card can sit in two rows at once, for example New and Jordan.
  • Focus starts on the first card in New. Each row remembers its last focused card.
  • Each card must still look right with no poster (a text-only title card).

Across every screen

  • Remote only: D-pad, Select and Back. One item always has focus (except on Pairing).
  • Offline: a small "offline" note in the corner, while the app keeps showing the last list it loaded.
  • Live updates: placeholder card → real card, without moving focus.
  • 10-foot rules: no text under 28 px at 1080p, safe margins on every edge, dark background.
  • Tone: calm and personal, closer to a notebook than a storefront. Services are plain text with no logos.
  • Original art: only the Recco mark and the empty states.
